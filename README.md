# Shadi Invitation — Sarvesh Soni & Sanjana Verma

A luxury, interactive digital Shadi invitation web application crafted in **React (JavaScript)**, **Tailwind CSS**, and **Framer Motion**.

---

## 💍 Event Details
- **Couple**: Sarvesh Soni & Sanjana Verma
- **Event**: Shadi (Wedding Ceremony)
- **Date**: Monday, 30 November 2026
- **Time**: 8:00 PM onwards
- **Venue**: Rudra Club n Resort, Kanpur Road, Lalganj

---

## ✨ Features

1. **Opening Envelope & Wax Seal Splash Screen**:
   - Dark espresso & burgundy embossed envelope with botanical relief patterns.
   - Central blush/rose-gold wax seal monogram (**S & S**).
   - Radial golden light burst and petal celebration upon clicking the wax seal.
   - 3D envelope flap unfolding transition into the main invitation.
   - Floating audio toggle button in the bottom-right corner.

2. **Hero & Shadi Invitation Section**:
   - Soft ivory/parchment background with watercolor/illustrated floral borders and corners (burgundy roses, blush blooms, golden eucalyptus).
   - Auspicious invocation (*॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥*), "Shadi" title badge, and date (30.11.26).
   - Romantic serif typography for **Sarvesh Soni & Sanjana Verma**.
   - Arched golden illustration featuring two swans facing each other on water forming a heart silhouette.
   - Warm Shadi invitation message and subtle animated "Scroll Down" prompt.

3. **Live Countdown Timer**:
   - Live ticker counting down to **November 30, 2026 at 8:00 PM**.
   - Displays Days, Hours, Minutes, and Seconds separated by colons over a layered hill silhouette.

4. **Venue & Interactive Map Section**:
   - "Location" heading with complete venue details.
   - Architectural palace illustration of **Rudra Club n Resort**.
   - Interactive styled map card with location pin and radar pulse.
   - "Open in Maps" (Google Maps navigation), "Add to Calendar" (Google Calendar reminder), and "Copy Address" with feedback.

5. **Dress Code Section**:
   - Guidance for guests to wear **Rose Gold**, **Burgundy**, and **Brown** tones.
   - Illustrated ensemble lineup of coordinated ethnic & formal attire (Lehenga, Sherwani, Tuxedo/Suit, Saree).
   - Interactive color swatches with hex codes and styling guidance.

6. **Interactive RSVP Section**:
   - Wax-seal badge to reveal the form.
   - Guest Name, Attendance (Accept with pleasure / Decline with regret), Number of guests attending, and optional blessing note.
   - Confetti burst on submission with personalized confirmation state saved to `localStorage`.
   - Romantic sign-off: *"Hope to see you then!"* and **Sarvesh Soni & Sanjana Verma** framed by soft floral accents.

7. **Ambient Wedding Music Engine**:
   - Web Audio API acoustic Indian classical Shehnai/harp romantic raga melody that plays without external MP3 dependencies.
